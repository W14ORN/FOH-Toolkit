FOH Toolkit Support

Help with accounts, shows, RTA, Ring Out, Community and App Store builds.

Contact support

FOH Toolkit support requests are handled through the project’s GitHub issue tracker. Do not include passwords, access tokens, private venue information or other sensitive data in a public issue.

Open a support request

Before reporting a problem

  • Confirm the version shown in Profile.
  • Fully close and reopen the installed app after an update.
  • Say whether you are online or offline and which iPhone/iPad or browser you are using.
  • For audio tools, include the input device and whether microphone permission was granted.
  • For console/show problems, include the desk model and selected stagebox/I/O profile.

Account deletion

Signed-in users can permanently delete their account in Profile → Account & Privacy → Delete account. You do not need to contact support to request normal account deletion.

Community safety

Use in-app report and block controls first where available. For urgent moderation issues that cannot be reported in the app, open a support request and identify the affected Community preset.
