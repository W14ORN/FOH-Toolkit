Privacy Policy

Effective: 1 October 2026

Short version: FOH Toolkit does not sell personal data, does not use advertising trackers, and does not upload microphone audio used by the RTA or Ring Out tools.

Information FOH Toolkit processes

  • Account information: email address, account ID and authentication records when you create an account.
  • Optional profile information: display name, company/band/venue and role.
  • Your saved work: shows, channel lists, console choice, patch plans, notes and app preferences when cloud sync is enabled.
  • Community activity: presets you submit, ratings, comments, reports, blocks and moderation records.
  • Operational metadata: timestamps needed to sync data and operate moderation features.

Microphone access

Microphone access is used only when you start the RTA or Ring Out tools. Audio is analysed locally on your device for frequency information. FOH Toolkit does not record, store or upload that microphone audio to its cloud database.

Guest mode

You can use core features without an account. Guest-mode shows and settings are stored locally on that device. If you clear app/browser data or uninstall before creating an account, local guest data may be lost.

Cloud services

FOH Toolkit uses Supabase for account authentication, cloud data storage and Community features. The web version is hosted with GitHub Pages. Those providers may process technical information needed to provide their services under their own privacy terms.

Why the data is used

Data is used to provide login, sync saved shows, restore settings across devices, operate Community features, prevent abuse, respond to reports and maintain the service. It is not used for third-party behavioural advertising.

Sharing

Community content you choose to publish may be visible to other users. FOH Toolkit does not sell personal information. Data may be processed by service providers that host or secure the service, or disclosed where legally required.

Retention and deletion

Account data is retained while your account exists. You can permanently delete your account from Profile → Account & Privacy → Delete account. Associated FOH Toolkit account state and user-generated Community content tied to the account are removed, except information that must be retained where required by law.

Security

FOH Toolkit uses authenticated access and database row-level security for cloud data. No online service can guarantee absolute security; report suspected issues through the Support page.

Your choices

You can use guest mode, decline microphone permission and continue using non-audio-analysis features, block Community users, report Community content, sign out, or delete your account.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the FOH Toolkit Support page.

This policy may be updated when the service changes. Material changes will be reflected by an updated effective date.
