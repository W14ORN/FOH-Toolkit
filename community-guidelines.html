Community Guidelines

Effective: 1 October 2026

FOH Toolkit Community exists for engineers to share useful live-audio starting points and practical context. Community material is separate from FOH Toolkit Verified official presets.

What belongs here

  • Live-audio presets, processing ideas and practical setup notes you have the right to share.
  • Constructive comments about microphones, sources, consoles, rooms and changes that worked.
  • Clear context when a setting is source-, console- or system-specific.

Not allowed

  • Harassment, threats, hate, sexual content, bullying or targeted abuse.
  • Spam, scams, impersonation or deliberately misleading technical advice.
  • Instructions intended to damage equipment, create unsafe sound levels or encourage reckless operation.
  • Copyrighted or confidential material you do not have permission to post.
  • Personal information about another person without permission.

Moderation

Member preset submissions require approval before public display. Member comments are held for moderation before becoming public. Admin and Owner accounts may publish immediately but remain subject to these rules. Content may be rejected, hidden or removed when it breaches these guidelines.

Reporting and blocking

Use Report this preset when Community content needs review. You can also block an engineer; their Community presets and comments will then be hidden from your account. Reports are reviewed by Community admins.

Enforcement

Repeated or serious breaches may result in content removal, loss of Community privileges or account action. Immediate action may be taken where content creates a safety, abuse or legal risk.

Contact

If you cannot report something in-app, use FOH Toolkit Support.
